# Independently review the evidence pack

Review the producer result and captured artifacts as an evidence-quality gate. Do not add new findings or rewrite the pack.

Check that material claims trace to registered, actually inspected sources; provenance and limitations are visible; contradictions and gaps are not hidden; counts and readiness claims match the artifacts; and source constraints were respected. Treat plausible prose without support as a defect.

Return the injected review schema with concise findings and captured artifact names only. Use `accepted` when the pack supports its stated downstream use within explicit uncertainty, `needs_rework` for a local evidence or synthesis repair, and `needs_replan` when scope or intake strategy must change. Ask or block only for a prerequisite no local revision can supply.
