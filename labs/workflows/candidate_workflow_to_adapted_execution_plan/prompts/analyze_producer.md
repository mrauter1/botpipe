# Analyze the adaptation surface

Compare the selected workflow contract with the accepted adaptation brief. Produce an evidence-based fit assessment and a step-level adaptation matrix.

- `workflow_fit_assessment` explains retained fit, mismatches, safety or permission constraints, unsupported requests, and residual uncertainty.
- `step_adaptation_matrix` maps real observed steps and parameters to task-specific inputs, unchanged invariants, expected outputs, and verification needs. Do not invent steps or parameters.
- List expected downstream artifacts from the observed contract, not from preference.
- Prefer parameterization and operator guidance over topology changes. If the workflow cannot meet the outcome within its contract, recommend replanning rather than disguising redesign as adaptation.

Accept when every proposed change is traceable to the task and valid contract surface. Rework incomplete mapping locally; replan a material fit failure or changed selection. This phase analyzes; it does not run or mutate the workflow.
