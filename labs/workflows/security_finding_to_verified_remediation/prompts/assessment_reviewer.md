Independently challenge exploitability, scope, threat assumptions, and acceptance criteria. Do not invent a new finding.

Verify that security claims trace to the adopted evidence pack; plausible attacker actions respect stated preconditions and trust boundaries; uncertainty affects posture; and acceptance criteria would demonstrate restoration of the security property.
