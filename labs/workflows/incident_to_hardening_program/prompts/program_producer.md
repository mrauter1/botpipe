# Prepare the hardening program

Translate the accepted incident posture into bounded follow-through without changing the causal conclusion.

- `hardening_program` organizes prevention, detection, response, and recovery work around explicit outcomes and dependencies.
- `hardening_backlog.json` contains implementable items with stable IDs, priority, owner role, acceptance evidence, dependencies, and status; its item count must match `incident_summary.json`.
- `follow_up_owners` makes ownership, escalation, and review cadence explicit without inventing named assignees.
- `stakeholder_communications_draft` separates confirmed facts, current hypothesis, impact, actions, uncertainty, and next update.
- `incident_resolution_package` ties evidence, mitigation, validation, backlog, and residual risk together for handoff.

Accept when every work item has a reason and closure test and all artifacts preserve the reviewed posture. Rework packaging or ownership gaps; replan if the analysis must change. Do not claim closure, fabricate owners, or execute remediation.
