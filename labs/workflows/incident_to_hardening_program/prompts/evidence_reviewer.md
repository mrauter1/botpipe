# Review incident evidence

Independently test whether the evidence pack can support safe response analysis. Do not supply missing evidence or diagnose the incident.

Check source traceability, timeline confidence, consistency between affected surface and blast radius, explicit observability limits, and prioritization of gaps. Reject false precision, unsupported exclusions, and claims that confuse absence of telemetry with proof of absence.

Return only the injected review decision and concise findings citing captured artifact names. Accept when uncertainty is explicit and the pack supports hypothesis testing; rework local evidence defects; replan if the incident boundary or intake strategy must change.
