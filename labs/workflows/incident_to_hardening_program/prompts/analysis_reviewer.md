# Review incident analysis

Independently challenge the causal ranking and immediate response posture. Do not replace the analyst's hypothesis set.

Check that the leading hypothesis survives meaningful alternatives and contradictory evidence; proposed tests can disconfirm it; mitigation is reversible where possible and does not outrun permissions; and the machine-readable posture agrees with the artifacts. A persuasive story without falsifiable evidence is not sufficient.

Return the injected review decision with concise, artifact-cited findings. Accept a defensible provisional analysis, rework local reasoning or safety gaps, and replan when evidence scope or incident framing must change.
