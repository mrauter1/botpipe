# Frame workflow evaluation

Keep the observed selected workflow fixed and define what meaningful evaluation must establish.

- `evaluation_request_brief` states the evaluation purpose, consumer, workflow identity, constraints, and intended use of results.
- `evaluation_dimensions` identifies workflow-specific quality, safety, robustness, and artifact expectations, including failures worth distinguishing.
- Treat optimizer-provided cases as development inputs, not withheld or already-passing evidence.

Accept when case design can proceed without changing the workflow or inventing its contract. Rework local ambiguity; replan a changed target or evaluation purpose; ask or block on a prerequisite that prevents meaningful scoring. Do not design cases or execute the workflow yet.
