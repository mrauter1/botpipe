# Frame an improvement cycle

Use observed company runs and the workflow catalog to define a bounded improvement review—not a mandate to improve everything.

- `company_operation_brief` identifies the scoped tasks and workflows, operational outcomes, recurring signals, evidence coverage, and important exclusions.
- `recursive_improvement_criteria` defines how to recognize leverage, urgency, confidence, cost, and safe ownership across the allowed improvement categories.
- Distinguish an observed pattern from a one-off event and record data gaps or sampling bias.

Accept when the scope and prioritization logic are usable. Rework local ambiguity, replan a materially changed scope, and ask or block only when evidence is too incomplete to prioritize responsibly. Do not propose or execute improvements yet.
