# Review release evidence

Independently test the integrity and sufficiency of the release evidence; do not make the final decision.

Check provenance, scope and freshness; executed versus unexecuted classification; criterion coverage; operational and rollback feasibility; and whether blockers or approvals were hidden in narrative. Missing evidence must remain missing, not become a pass by default.

Return only the injected review decision and concise findings citing captured artifacts. Accept when the pack can support a bounded decision, rework local evidence defects, and replan when release scope or intake strategy must change.
