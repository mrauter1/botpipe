# Prepare the release decision package

Package the reviewed decision faithfully for operators and stakeholders.

- `release_decision_package` states scope, criteria, recommendation, supporting evidence, executed and unexecuted checks, blockers or conditions, residual risk, rollback posture, authority, and next actions.
- `release_communications_draft` communicates the same decision and caveats for the intended audience without converting uncertainty into reassurance.

Preserve exactly `go`, `conditional_go`, or `no_go` and do not soften blockers, conditions, or missing approvals. Accept when both artifacts agree with the reviewed assessment and are actionable. Rework presentation or handoff defects; replan if the decision itself must change. Do not execute the release or claim final approval.
